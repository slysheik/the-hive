# DEEP RESEARCH DIRECTIVE — Platform Exhaustion Template

Run this in Claude (paste the platform's ToS) AND in Perplexity (live web search) for every platform before listing it.

Never list a platform you haven't run through this template.

---

```
RESEARCH TARGET: [Platform name and URL]

Investigate every dimension below. Do not summarise — go deep on each. 
If information is unavailable, say "not found" — do not guess or assume.

---

1. THE OFFER MECHANICS
   - Exact deposit minimum and maximum (in USD and KES equivalent)
   - Exact bonus amount and how it is credited (cash, tokens, points, credits?)
   - Is the bonus withdrawable immediately or locked?
   - Are there multiple offer tiers? List all.

2. THE WITHDRAWAL REALITY
   - Every fee associated with withdrawing (flat, percentage, or both — list all)
   - Minimum withdrawal amount
   - Withdrawal methods available in Kenya specifically
   - M-Pesa compatibility: direct / via crypto / not available
   - Average real withdrawal time (not what they claim — what users in forums report)
   - Maximum withdrawal per day/week/month

3. THE HIDDEN CONDITIONS
   - Wagering or activity requirements before withdrawal is possible
   - Time limits on completing requirements (deadline before bonus expires)
   - Geographic restrictions: is Kenya explicitly included, explicitly excluded, or not mentioned?
   - KYC requirements: what documents, estimated processing time in Kenya
   - Any conditions that void the bonus (e.g., using VPN, withdrawing before activity threshold)
   - Any platform-specific definitions that redefine "withdrawal" or "bonus" in unusual ways

4. THE PLATFORM'S REPUTATION
   - Years in operation
   - Any history of payment delays, frozen accounts, or changed terms without notice
   - Reddit, Trustpilot, and forum sentiment — surface specific user complaints, not just ratings
   - Any known issues specific to African or Kenyan users
   - Response time of support when users have problems

5. THE REAL NUMBERS
   - What do actual users report earning per hour (in USD, convert to KES for output)
   - What is the realistic net profit after all fees in KES?
   - What percentage of users who attempt the offer actually complete it successfully?
   - Is there evidence of users being denied payouts despite completing requirements?

6. THE AFFILIATE / REFERRAL PROGRAM
   - Does an affiliate program exist?
   - Commission structure: per signup, per deposit, revenue share, token-based?
   - Payment method for affiliate earnings: is it Kenya-accessible?
   - Minimum payout threshold for affiliate earnings
   - Any restrictions on promotion method (e.g., no paid ads, no coupon sites)
   - Application requirements: minimum traffic, social following, existing site?

7. RED FLAGS
   - Every ToS clause that could be used to deny a payout
   - Any clause allowing them to change terms retroactively without notice
   - Any reports of accounts being banned specifically for bonus hunting or legitimate completion
   - Any pattern of complaints about KYC being used to delay or deny payouts indefinitely

---

FINAL OUTPUT (required — do not skip):

RISK RATING: [Low / Medium / High]

RECOMMENDATION: [List it / List with warnings / Do not list]

THE ONE THING a Kenyan user must know before signing up: [One sentence. The most important single fact.]

KES NET CALCULATION:
- Offer: [deposit amount in KES] → [bonus in KES]
- Withdrawal fee: [KES amount]
- Conversion chain loss: [estimated KES amount]
- NET PROFIT: [KES amount]
- If time required: hourly rate = [KES/hr]
```

---

## When to Run This

- Before adding any platform to the aggregator
- When a platform's terms are flagged as changed by the crawler
- When a user reports a problem with a listed platform
- At quarterly review of all active offers

## Where to Run It

- **Perplexity:** For live web results, Reddit sentiment, current user reports
- **Claude:** For ToS analysis (paste the actual ToS text into Claude with this prompt)
- Run both. Combine outputs. Never rely on just one.
